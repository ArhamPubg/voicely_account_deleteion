Account & Data Deletion

Voicely – VoIP Calling Application

At Voicely, we respect your right to control your personal data. In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, this page explains how you can permanently delete your Voicely account and all associated data.

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Voicely app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile / Settings screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.
  4. Your account and data will be permanently wiped immediately.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

Send an email to: arhamshahzad.dev@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Voicely

Body: Please include the registered phone number associated with your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • User Profile: Your registered phone number and user ID.
  • Call Records & History: All incoming, outgoing, and missed call logs.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging push notification tokens.
  • Authentication Credentials: Your Firebase Auth session and identity records.

Retention Period

Upon receiving your request, all personal data is permanently deleted from our servers within 48 hours. No user data is retained after deletion.
